How to read the table

  • Privacy risk — Low = no camera, or a documented local path (Valetudo root / no-cloud model); Medium = camera + vendor cloud, or camera-free but cloud-bound; High = camera + cloud + a poor security track record (Ecovacs after the 2024 DEF CON research and hijack incidents). Expand a row for exactly what the camera does with images and where the cloud lives.
  • Cloud required means mapping, scheduling or starting a clean needs the vendor app/cloud. Local-capable rows either need no app at all (bump-and-go budget bots) or can be rooted with Valetudo for fully offline control. Matter-only robots still need the vendor app for maps, so they stay cloud-required.
  • Navigation — LiDAR maps rooms without imaging them; a camera adds object recognition (cables, pet waste) at the cost of a lens pointed at your floor. Obstacle-avoidance rating is the practical outcome.
  • Pickup % are RTINGS-style deep-clean figures from memory (±8 pts) and n/a for the newest models; suction Pa is the marketing number and correlates loosely.
  • Dock tags: auto-empty (dust), mop wash/dry (hot water + heated drying), water refill (plumbed or tank). The full-dock flagships cost $1,000+.
