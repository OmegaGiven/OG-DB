How to read the table

  • Multi-colour tells you the mechanism, which decides waste and speed: Toolchanger (Prusa XL, Snapmaker U1) and Dual nozzle (H2D, IDEX) switch heads with no purge; Multi-spool + purge (Bambu AMS, Creality CFS, Anycubic ACE, Qidi Box) and MMU (Prusa MMU3) feed one nozzle and purge each change — expect a poop-bucket and 2–4× print time on colour-heavy models. Max colours is with the largest first-party setup.
  • Hotend / extruder — direct drive handles TPU and fast retractions; Bowden is lighter but fussier. Nozzle max °C gates materials (300 °C+ for PA / PC / CF blends); the nozzle-change note says whether it's a quick-swap module or a wrench job.
  • Enclosure matters for ABS / ASA / PA; a heated chamber (H2D, Core One, X1E, Qidi Plus 4) is the step above for warp-free engineering plastics.
  • Cloud — LAN-only OK means the printer runs without the vendor cloud. Bambu's Jan-2025 firmware change put third-party slicer control behind Bambu Connect unless you enable Developer / LAN mode; Prusa, Klipper machines and kits are fully local.
  • $ / L under build volume is price per litre of print space — the honest big-printer comparison.
